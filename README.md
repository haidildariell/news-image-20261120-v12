One broadsheet that sums up Friday 20 November 2026: 36 dated stories
across 7 regions — Kyiv's bridges hit, Cyclone Akvan's landfall in
Odisha, Asia's AI sell-off, COP31's deadlocked final day — plus a UTC
timeline of the day, a numbered world map, and a DATE CHECK box that
keeps Thursday's news out.

`build_image.py` draws the page (Python + Pillow, deterministic, no
network) into `entry.png`; `main.py` (standard library only) copies that
image to the output directory. Every story is sourced in `SOURCES.md`.
